the ship criterion. `check grain-shape` finds a headerless doc and then names a command that, for

gate helper also lets a grain document impersonate a shared doc, which is rule 4's first sin.

  absolute tree path (`:11-13`), as the ship criterion says.

## Dispositions

Every BLOCKER, CRITICAL and MAJOR carries a disposition; MINOR and NIT stay
`open` by design — severity gates the hold, and the rest are recorded and
carried rather than fixed under a close.

**What was deferred, and why each one is safe to carry.** None is a behaviour
that is wrong today; each is a PROOF gap or a second spelling of a fact the
tree already states, and all are filed against `ms-0.5.0`:
