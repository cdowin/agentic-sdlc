## Findings

### The root cause behind B1, B2, C1 and C2

`check pm` has two kinds of walk. `model.pool_walk` sees every document; the roll-ups descend by
binding (`model.milestones` → `feature_files(mid)` → `story_files(fid)` → `bug_files(mid)`).
`f0a58b5` added ONE compensating pass, `checks/pm.py:189 _unreached_self`, and it is short in two
ways: it applies only D4 and the READY warnings (not D1, not V4/V5, not U1, not bugs), and its
`reached()` predicate at `checks/pm.py:201-205` asks *"does my binding resolve to a grain of the
right kind"* when the question it stands in for is *"did the descent actually reach me"*. Those are
different for a story bound to a feature that is itself unbound: the story is skipped by
`_unreached_self` **and** never visited by `_drift_walk`.
