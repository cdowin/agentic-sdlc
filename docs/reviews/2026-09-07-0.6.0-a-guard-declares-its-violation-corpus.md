## The design follows the shipped precedent rather than inventing one

`prepare-commit-msg --self-test` replays its own cases and `check hooks` counts which hooks have one.
Here a guard declares `CORPUS = ((planted, must_be_caught), …)` and `catches(planted) -> bool`, and
the gate replays it. Two details earn their place: a corpus must hold **at least one violation AND
at least one clean case** (an all-clean corpus passes over a blind guard), and where a rich table
already existed the corpus is **derived** from it (`CORPUS = OPEN_SPELLINGS`) rather than copied —
a second copy would be the scoreboard this milestone deletes everywhere else.

The gate is itself an AST-shaped guard and replays its own 9-case corpus.
