## What is genuinely covered

`ledger.ROW_KEYS` (every key routes or renders), `EVENT_KEYS` (every shape opens on the stamp),
`LESSON_KEYS`, `changelog.COLUMNS` (row width and `--json` keys are one declaration),
`cli.LIST_COLUMNS` (no duplicate column, every listable kind declared), `model.BINDS_TO` (every
binding field is one the shipped template actually carries).

`report.TOTAL_KEY is ledger.TOTAL_KEY` is asserted with `is`, not `==`: two string literals that
happen to match is the `at`/`ts` defect waiting for one of them to be edited.
