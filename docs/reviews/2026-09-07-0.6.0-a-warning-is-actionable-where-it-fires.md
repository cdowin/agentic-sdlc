## The judgement this rests on, and it is the right one

45 of 57 warnings fired on grains in the `done` category. A `## Ship criterion` on a shipped grain
is retro-fiction — the rung where writing one still changes an outcome is `in_progress`, and that is
where the family now fires. **This is D12's boundary, already drawn in this module hours earlier for
the same reason**: 168 grains closed before the `changelog:` field existed and asking them all for a
sentence nobody will write is the same defect. Two rules reaching the same line independently is the
argument that the line is in the right place.
