**Verdict: SHIP-WITH-FIXES.** Two MAJORs, both in the courier/hand join the report inherits from

## Blockers

- **F1 MAJOR** `src/agentic_sdlc/repo/pm/report.py:577-586`: `_folded` puts the hand row's grain,
  spend, counts and `agent_type` onto the courier twin, but not its `outcome` or `issue`. A
  dispatch recorded with `--outcome landed` then prints `-` once its courier row is joined. Probe:
  courier row `agent_id yyyPROBE` in the root ledger plus a hand row `{grain, agent_id yyyPROBE,
  outcome: landed, issue: [41]}` in the milestone ledger gives a unit printing `-  developer … 7777 -`
  (no issue, no outcome). That is what happens to all six of 0.10.0's own builder rows, which were
  hand-recorded with `landed`, once their couriers' rows merge in. **Fix:** fold `OUTCOME_FIELD`
  and `ISSUE_FIELD` from the hand row when the courier has none, and amend the existing join case.
- **F2 MAJOR** `src/agentic_sdlc/repo/pm/report.py:1034` + `:1265` + `:718`: twins are joined per
  report, over (this milestone's ledger + the root ledger) only. The courier row is placed by its
  own `branch`, and wherever the hand row is absent it stays an unjoined dispatch, so one dispatch
  gets counted in two reports. Probe: a courier row on `branch: milestone/0.9.0-…` (5000 tokens)
  plus its hand twin in 0.10.0's ledger. `report ms-the-ledger-is-a-stamp` prints unit 9 at 5000,
  `report ms-the-tool-agrees-with-itself` prints unit 4 at 5000, and the comparison's `units` block
  carries it in both rows. With the courier on `main`, it is in 0.10.0's units and in `--tree`'s
  `dispatch` count as well (31→32). This breaks "a comparison shares no row" and "hand-recording
  adds no second dispatch". **Fix:** join twins across every ledger in the tree before any
  placement, in `build` and `tree_data` alike, so the hand row's grain places the pair everywhere.

## Per grain

**ft-the-flow-is-boring-by-construction.** `cc-git-allowlist` blocked 23 of 23 typed bypass shapes
(`-C`, `cd &&`, `/usr/bin/git`, `command`, `env`, `time`, `nice`, `xargs`, `-c k=v`, `HEAD:main`,
`+ref`, `--mirror`), passed the add/commit/push flow, and its self-test passes (41 blocked, 46
allowed). `cc-agent-isolation` refuses both tool names and names `agent-worktree.sh`.
`dispatch --mode parallel` renders the loop against `branch:`, and `--mode bogus` is exit 2. The
installed copies match their sources outside the header. The leader write-confinement clause was
not built, which was deliberate, **but the feature file does not say so** (W3).

**ft-a-milestone-reports-only-its-own-rows.** `owns()` is clean: a grain is decided by the grain
alone, then the branch, then a snapshot naming exactly one story. Two milestones with distinct
grains and branches cannot share a row, except through the twin join (F2).

**ft-work-is-stamped-with-its-issue-and-agent.** The criterion was replayed verbatim and met. Stop
with no start is exit 1 and writes nothing. Every row gains `branch`.

**ft-a-concurrent-dispatch-attributes-itself.** A `GDK-STAMP` line renders into the prompt and
`record --from-transcript` copies it back (`test_dispatch.py:217`). A second dispatch fails through
F2. The preamble still renders `export GDK_LEDGER_GRAIN` (see the milestone record).

**ft-the-report-is-the-stamp-table.** The report prints `units`, `by agent`, `time per state` and
the counted lines. `--help` names every block's columns in order. `report.py` went from 2167 to
1476 lines (−691).

**ft-a-dispatch-row-carries-its-outcome.** `--outcome` validates (`stopped:` with no reason is exit
2). The superseded-spend line totals and names the grains (planted 900 tokens on
`st-a-deleted-pilot`, printed). It loses its outcome on a join (F1). **Its `## Ship criterion` is
still the placeholder** (W4). I measured it against its `changelog:`.

**bg-an-unknown-agent-type-is-recorded-as-a-dispatch.** `record --agent-type wombat`,
`stamp --agent wombat`: both exit 2 naming the twelve. **bg-two-gate-runs-share-one-log.** Each
run gets its own `<slot>.<pid>` and renames it onto the slot. The barrier test
`test_two_concurrent_runs_each_count_only_what_they_ran` covers it.
